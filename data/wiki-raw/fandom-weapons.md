## Melee — general mechanics
- Melee use stamina; swing in an arc, can hit multiple zombies.
- Hold mouse button to CHARGE: more damage, slower startup.
- Edged weapons: more likely to sever limbs. Blunt: increased headshot damage when charged.
- Blunt theme = higher knockdown at higher tiers (e.g. Sledgehammer).
- Sharp theme = more damage at higher tiers (e.g. Fire Axe).
- One-handed melee/firearms let you hold flashlight in offhand without lowering weapon.

## Melee — Two-Handed
- 2H deal more damage than 1H, slower swing, more stamina; very wide arc; flashlight lowered.
- Rebar — Blunt. Weakest 2H, 2H upgrade over Small Pipe. Kills normal zombie w/ 2 charged head hits.
- T-junction Pipe — Blunt. Variant of Rebar.
- Giant Wrench — Blunt. Variant of Rebar.
- Baseball Bat — Blunt. Knocks zombies over.
- Sledgehammer — Blunt. Knocks over w/ 1 hit. Kills normal zombie w/ 1 charged head hit.
- Fire Axe — Sharp. High damage, occasionally 1 charged head hit kill.
- Shovel — WIP.
- Tree Trimmer — Sharp. Great reach, dismember legs -> crawlers w/ charged attacks.

## Firearms (by caliber) — display name, mag, notes
- 9mm:
  - M93A3 (aka M9A3, Beretta M9 model) — mag 15. 2 headshots to kill normal (1 w/ pistol-damage perk). Offhand flashlight. Fits Pistol Suppressor + Micro Red Dot.
  - MP5 — mag 30. Built-in flashlight (no batteries). Fits Rifle Suppressor + Rifle Optics.
- .45 ACP:
  - M1911 — mag 7+1. 1 headshot kill. Offhand flashlight. Fits Pistol Suppressor.
- .357 Magnum:
  - Model 13 — mag 6. Default starting ranged weapon. 1 headshot normal, 2 for red (Prime). Offhand flashlight.
  - Rochester 1873 — lever action .357 rifle. mag 5. Fits Rifle Suppressor + Rifle optics.
- .308 Win:
  - Hunter 85 — bolt action. mag 6. 1 headshot normal, 2 red. Fits Rifle Suppressor.
  - M14 Battle Rifle — semi-auto upgrade to Hunter 85, same damage, bigger mag. mag 20+1. Fits Rifle Suppressor + Holographic Sight.
- 6.8x51mm:
  - M7A1 — modern army rifle, full auto. mag 20+1. 1 headshot normal AND red. Fits Rifle Suppressor, Long Gun Flashlight, Holographic Sight.
- 5.56mm:
  - MC-15 — civilian semi-auto rifle. mag 30+1. 1 headshot normal, 2 red. Fits Rifle Suppressor, Long Gun Flashlight, Holographic Sight.
- 12 Gauge:
  - Fierra DB12 — over-under double barrel. mag 2. 1 headshot normal, 2 red. Can sever limbs (legs->crawlers).
  - 590A1 — pump action tube-fed upgrade over DB12. mag 7.

## Attachments
- Optics: Micro Red Dot (pistol; M9A3, M1911). Holographic Sight, ACOG, Long Gun Scope (rifle; MP5, Rochester 1873, M7A1, MC-15, 590A1).
- Suppressors: tied to weapon type not caliber. Pistol supp: M9A3, M1911. Rifle supp: MP5, Rochester 1873, Hunter 85, M14, M7A1, MC-15, Gruber Ranch. Un-suppressible: Model 13, Fierra DB12, 590A1.
- Rail: Gun-Lights only (as of 15 Apr 2025). Long gun flashlight: M7A1, MC-15, Gruber Ranch. MP5 has built-in.
