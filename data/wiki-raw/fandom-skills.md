Source: https://no-more-room-in-hell-two.fandom.com/wiki/Skills  (CC-BY-SA)

Mechanics: each Responder has a starter skill (from pre-outbreak job) + 7 slots = 8 total.
At Responder levels 5,10,15,20,30,40,50 pick 1 of 3 random skills. Each skill has an
"Expert" version (= our from-files "_Ultimate") with an ACCOUNT-level requirement.
